# Security, resilience and observability

Bezpečnost, resilience a observabilita jsou průřezové quality attributes. Urči trust boundaries, identity, autorizaci, citlivost dat, audit, secrets, threat scenarios a incident ownership.

U integrací popiš timeout, retry, circuit breaking, idempotency, dead-letter, compensation a reconciliation. Observability musí propojit business událost, correlation ID, technické metriky, logy, traces a alert ownership.
